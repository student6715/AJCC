# Problem Solving Format

## Basic information
- 2 hours
- The marks are given out of total 100
- Partial points are given
- A chess board and pieces are provided
- Two sets of questions for U12 and U18
- In U12, prizes are given for U8, U10, and U12 for boys and girls
- In U18, prizes are given for U14, U16, and U18 for boys and girls
